1. Who We Are

Dedicated Road Safety Manufacturing

Based in Pune, Maharashtra, BR Enterprises specializes in engineering and supplying reliable road safety infrastructure across India. We cater to commercial hubs, industrial facilities, residential townships, and road development projects.

Our focus is straightforward: providing durable, tested road safety assets—from heavy-duty modular rubber speed breakers and high-rebound delineators to reflective road studs and barrier systems—built to endure heavy traffic and harsh weather conditions.

Engineered for Durability

Heavy-duty rubber and plastic polymers formulated to withstand extreme traffic loads and UV exposure.

Quality Benchmarked

Strictly compliant with IRC guidelines and BIS specifications for municipal and corporate approvals.

Our Mission & Vision
