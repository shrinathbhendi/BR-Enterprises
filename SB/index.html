About BR Enterprises

India's Leading Road Safety Product Manufacturer

BR Enterprises is a trusted manufacturer, supplier, exporter, and dealer of premium road safety equipment. Established with a vision to make Indian roads safer, we deliver quality-tested products that meet national and international standards.

Our comprehensive product range includes Rubber Speed Breakers, Plastic Speed Breakers, Traffic Cones, Convex Mirrors, Cat Eye Reflectors, Solar Cat Eyes, Boom Barriers, Road Barriers, Crash Barriers, Steel Bollards, Queue Managers, Rubber Corner Guards, Thermoplastic Road Marking Paints, and Traffic Safety Signage.
